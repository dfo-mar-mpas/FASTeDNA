### FASTeDNA: a field-deployable, adaptable eDNA workflow for rapid detection and real-time aquatic surveillance 

#### Meghan C. McBride, Evan Cronmiller, Robert Bajno, Genevieve J. Parent, Nellie Gagne, Francis LeBlanc, Nicholas W. Jeffery, Kira Krumhansl, Sarah Kingsbury, Ryan R.E. Stanley, Anais Lacoursiere-Roussel, Anne-Laure Ferchaud, Claudio DiBacco 

**Abstract**

Environmental DNA (eDNA) has emerged as a powerful tool for monitoring and surveillance of aquatic invasive species (AIS). However, conventional workflows rely on centralized laboratory processing which can introduce delays in result turnaround, limiting its effectiveness where time-sensitive decisions are required. Here, we introduce FASTeDNA, a field-deployable targeted qPCR-based eDNA workflow for the rapid (4–5 hours) detection of marine and freshwater aquatic species. This workflow supports point-of-need applications aimed at identifying and reducing introduction risk, including watercraft inspections at point-of-entry locations, vessel biofouling assessments, regulatory enforcement, and remote monitoring. We developed and tested the protocol, consisting of DNA extraction and real-time PCR, with field samples targeting high-risk marine (Ciona intestinalis, Botryllus schlosseri, Botrylloides violaceus) and freshwater (Dreissena polymorpha) AIS across a range of abundances, yielding accurate, reliable, and repeatable results comparable to those obtained through laboratory-based analyses. The protocol was subsequently evaluated by individuals with a range of molecular laboratory experience, all of whom produced accurate results and provided feedback to improve overall user-friendliness. Additional field trials conducted by end users not involved in the development confirmed the accuracy and broader applicability of the FASTeDNA workflow into existing AIS, Species-at-risk and conservation area monitoring programs across species (Procambaus clarkii, Sargassum muticum, Hemigrapsus sanguineus, Anarhichas lupus). Collectively, these results demonstrate that FASTeDNA provides a rapid, reliable, and accessible tool for real-time aquatic surveillance. By complementing existing laboratory infrastructure while expanding monitoring capacity and accessibility, FASTeDNA enables more timely decision-making for biosecurity, conservation, and environmental management in both marine and freshwater ecosystems.
